Quadrotor navigation & obstacle avoidance

Dalian Maritime University · Co-author on an accepted ICRA 2026 paper

Implemented artificial potential field obstacle avoidance in V-REP using Lua and explored ant colony and particle swarm optimization for quadrotor path planning. Integrated Crazyflie, distance sensing, and ROS-based flight control for indoor experiments, using NOKOV motion capture to inspect trajectories and debug the flight system.
